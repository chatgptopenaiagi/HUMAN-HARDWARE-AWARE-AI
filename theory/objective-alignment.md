# Objective alignment
## Local versus global success
Docker installation, Python configuration and successful compilation are local outcomes. If the human still cannot perform the desired workflow under their operating conditions, the mission remains incomplete.

## Proposed mechanism
Maintain a requirement-to-action-to-evidence trace. After each meaningful action, compare results against the current version of the human objective. Mark each mandatory criterion satisfied, failed, unknown or blocked. Preserve the original contract and authorized amendments.

A mission completes only when every mandatory criterion has valid evidence and unresolved defects do not contradict acceptance. Optional improvements do not justify endless execution. A blocked objective pauses with an explicit resumption condition.

## Research question
Does repeated objective comparison reduce drift enough to offset its overhead? Evaluate with independent acceptance review, including apparently successful local actions that fail the whole workflow.

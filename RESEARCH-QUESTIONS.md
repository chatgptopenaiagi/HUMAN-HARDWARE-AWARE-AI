# Research questions
## Foundational questions
1. **What does the human want?** Can an acceptance contract preserve intent through long sequences and revisions?
2. **What does the human have?** How can inventory remain useful while distinguishing current verification from historical observations?
3. **What can the whole system accomplish?** Which small integration probes establish useful combined capabilities?

## Investigations
- Does persistent evidence reduce duplicate work without excessive maintenance cost?
- When does capability discovery cost more than the work it avoids?
- How should freshness depend on volatility and consequence?
- Can consequence forecasts reduce failed changes at a tolerable false-alarm rate?
- Under what conditions does parallel execution improve completed outcomes after coordination costs?
- Can local success metrics conceal objective failure?
- How do strategies vary with power, memory, network and human time constraints?

## Falsification
An integrated approach may be slower or more expensive on short tasks. Stale memory may increase errors. Discovery can become intrusive or irrelevant. Forecasts may block feasible actions. Experiments must report these outcomes and unsuccessful missions rather than selecting only favorable demonstrations.

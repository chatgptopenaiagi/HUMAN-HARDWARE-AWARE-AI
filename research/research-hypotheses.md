# Research hypotheses
All entries below are untested proposals. GENESIS-1 provides no effect estimates.

| ID | Hypothesis | Comparison and falsification |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Versioned intent reduces objective drift | Compare acceptance failures with and without contracts; equal or worse results weaken H1 |
| H2 | Fresh capability evidence reduces repeated failures | Compare failure/retry rates while counting probe overhead |
| H3 | Shared memory reduces duplicate operations | Compare independently classified duplication and maintenance cost |
| H4 | Hardware-aware planning improves feasible completion | Compare equal-quality missions across hardware classes |
| H5 | Consequence evaluation improves recovery | Compare failure severity, false alarms and recovery time |
| H6 | Coordination improves useful parallelism | Compare serial and isolated parallel tasks including coordination cost |
| H7 | Integrated workflow recovers human time | Compare active human minutes at equal acceptance quality |
| H8 | Reuse lowers energy per completed objective | Compare measured energy within a declared boundary; include failed attempts |

## Interpretation
A favorable result on one task or device does not establish general superiority. Negative findings, ceiling effects and intervention overhead must be published. Benefits may depend on task duration, environment volatility and operator experience.

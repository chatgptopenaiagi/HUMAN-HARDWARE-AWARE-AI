# Vision
The human defines the intended outcome and retains authority to change it. Engineering should begin with that outcome, an inventory of actual resources, and evidence about how those resources work together.

The original proposition in this mission is an integrated objective-resource-capability process with persistent memory and explicit completion checks. It is a research framing, not a claim that its constituent ideas are new. Inventory, provenance, planning, testing and coordination already have established approaches.

A phone, tablet, notebook, workstation and computing cluster may pursue the same product through different execution strategies. The system should choose from feasible options, explain tradeoffs, and stop when acceptance evidence establishes completion.

## Governance
Present current state, planned actions, expected consequences, uncertainty, verified achievements and remaining work in concise language. Escalate missing authority, material ambiguity or irreversible consequences; do not repeatedly ask for decisions already made. Treat attention as a finite resource.

## Success boundary
A successful installation is an intermediate result. A successful mission satisfies the human's functional and operational conditions. Proposed time and resource benefits must survive measurement, including discovery, supervision and recovery overhead.

# HUMAN-HARDWARE-AWARE-AI
## GENESIS-1: theoretical foundation
**What does the human want? What does the human have? What can the whole system accomplish?**

Artificial intelligence must adapt its engineering strategy to the human's actual resources and desired outcome, rather than requiring the human to repeatedly reconstruct their environment around the AI.

This repository proposes human-hardware capability alignment: preserve the human objective, verify available resources and combined capabilities, and select a feasible strategy with observable completion conditions. The objective is verified human achievement, not maximum autonomous activity.

## Research status
GENESIS-1 established a conceptual research foundation. [GENESIS-1.1](genesis-1.1/README.md) adds an experimental Awareness Kernel with deterministic simulated transitions and Windows-enforced proposer restrictions. It is not a production orchestration engine. Proposed benefits remain untested unless explicitly identified otherwise. No performance, energy, economic benefit or scientific novelty is claimed.

## Installable developer preview
[GENESIS-1.2](genesis-1.2/README.md) packages the existing kernel as an **EXPERIMENTAL DEVELOPER PREVIEW**: a Python wheel and a Windows console executable for snapshot inspection, capability graphs and transition evaluation. Proposed actions do not execute in these distributions. Read the [build report](genesis-1.2/BUILD-REPORT.md) and [distribution boundary](genesis-1.2/SECURITY.md).

[GENESIS-1.3](genesis-1.3/README.md) adds evidence-backed capability sufficiency, goal-conditioned read-only probes and persistent human Intent revision. The modification gateway remains disabled; application-owned state and bounded read-only observations are supported. Earlier research and releases remain preserved.
